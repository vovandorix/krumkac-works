MY GARDEN

A mobile app for organising garden work, caring for plants and keeping a history of your garden.

A more organised approach to gardening

MY GARDEN brings together the tools for planning garden work, caring for plants and keeping track of your garden throughout the season.

  • A personal collection of plants with categories and planting locations
  • Garden work planning, seasonal recommendations and reminders
  • Individual plant records with photos, notes and a history of care
  • A garden calendar with useful seasonal and lunar guidance
  • Daily overview of planned garden tasks
