MOVA

Learn Belarusian through vocabulary, dialogues, audio, grammar and games.

Learn Belarusian step by step

MOVA brings together vocabulary, dialogues, audio, grammar and games to help you explore and practise the Belarusian language.

  • Thematic dictionaries and vocabulary collections
  • Flashcards with translations and phonetic transcriptions
  • Vocabulary review and memorisation
  • Everyday dialogues with audio and translations
  • Games for vocabulary practice
  • Grammar rules and exercises
  • Synonyms and favourite words
  • Learning progress, statistics and achievements
  • Themes and interface language settings
  • Progress backup through data export and import

Free to use and without advertising.
